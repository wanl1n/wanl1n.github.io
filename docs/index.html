About Me

I'm a game developer and artist, recently graduated Summa Cum Laude in Game Development at De La Salle University. My passion for creativity and leadership motivates me to create and innovate in all my projects.

I led teams of up to 14 members across diverse disciplines — game development, software programming, marketing, and graphic design — honing my project management and leadership skills along the way.

I've worked on both the development and marketing side, launching and managing social media channels for organizations like the Lasallian Scholars Society and my own project, Largo's Labyrinth.

My passion ultimately drives me to create memorable and immersive experiences with the help of my technical expertise, creativity, and thirst for new experiences.
